# Founder's Story — Peak Fuel Foods

*This is a fictional company created for SCM 478. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.*

---

## The Origin

Peak Fuel Foods was founded in 2018 by Marcus and Elena Vance, two former collegiate athletes who were frustrated with what they found on supplement store shelves: overpriced products with undisclosed sourcing, artificial everything, and no story behind them.

"We wanted to know exactly what we were putting in our bodies," Elena recalled. "And we wanted other athletes to have that same confidence."

Operating out of a rented commercial kitchen in Boise, Idaho, they started with a single product: a whey protein blend made entirely from dairy farms within 200 miles. They sold it at farmers markets and CrossFit gyms. Within six months, they had a waitlist.

---

## Growing Pains

By 2020, Peak Fuel had five products and a problem: the spreadsheets weren't working anymore.

Marcus was tracking purchase orders in one file, inventory counts in another, and sales in a third. None of them talked to each other. When a supplier was delayed, he found out at the loading dock. When a product ran low, he didn't know until a customer complained.

"We knew we had a supply chain," he said. "We just didn't have visibility into it."

---

## Where They Are Now

Peak Fuel Foods now distributes to 40+ retailers across the Pacific Northwest, with a growing direct-to-consumer channel. They have eight SKUs, a team of twelve, and a warehouse in Meridian, Idaho.

They still don't have an ERP system. Instead, they have a set of carefully maintained CSV files and a Streamlit dashboard — one that a BYU-Idaho intern built during a summer practicum.

That dashboard is the starting point for your work in SCM 478.

---

## Your Role

You are a supply chain analyst joining Peak Fuel Foods as a summer intern. Your job is to build out and improve their operational systems, starting with what they have and adding capabilities week by week.

By the end of the semester, you will hand Marcus and Elena a system that can:

- Display and filter their full product and ingredient catalog
- Track sales by channel and product over time
- Monitor purchase orders and flag potential shortages
- Forecast demand and archive projections
- Identify production bottlenecks
- Explode their BOMs for production planning

They're counting on you.
